# Third-party parser

The ICU MessageFormat 1 adapter includes unmodified parser files from
[SirStendec/pyicumessageformat](https://github.com/SirStendec/pyicumessageformat)
at commit `770de136b9b526c0baaf889d023c115999962430` (package 1.0.0).
The MIT copyright and permission text are preserved in each vendored package's LICENSE.
Neo Korean Writing's adapter, policy checks and regressions are separate original code.
No translation model, remote service or Weblate runtime is bundled.
